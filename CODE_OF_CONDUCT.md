# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and keep discussion on topic. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ardihikaru) or a private security advisory on this
repository. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, issues or contributions and may block participants who violate this
code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
