# Design sitemap

Approved design documents for Pustaka, newest first. A design lands here in
the same pull request that a human approves it in; drafts and review reports
stay in `.dev/docs/` (git-ignored). See `AGENTS.md` → *Elephant-Goldfish
workflow* for when a design is required.

| Design | Status | Area | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Open-source readiness and the Elephant-Goldfish workflow](oss-and-egm.md) | Approved | repo, AI tooling, auth | Licensing and community files, CI, generic deploy manifests, EGM roles for the authoring loop, and trusted-proxy keying for the login throttle. |
